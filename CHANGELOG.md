# Changelog

## 0.1.1

- **The Golden Raptor trophy can be placed:** it is now a block, a gold eagle with raised wings on a wooden stand that faces you and glows softly.

## 0.1.0

The first version, for Bedrock and Java Edition.

- **22 birds of prey:** golden eagle, bald eagle, white-tailed eagle, Steller's sea eagle, osprey, peregrine falcon, gyrfalcon, kestrel, northern goshawk, sparrowhawk, red kite, common buzzard, Harris's hawk, marsh harrier, barn owl, tawny owl, snowy owl, great grey owl, Eurasian eagle-owl, secretary bird, Andean condor and lammergeier. Each has its own model, plumage, flight and calls.
- **Hunting styles:** falcons stoop, kestrels and ospreys hover, ospreys plunge for fish, eagles and vultures soar, owls hunt at night, Harris's hawks hunt in families, the secretary bird stamps on snakes, the lammergeier drops bones, scavengers gather where a big animal has died.
- **Falconry:** tame with raw meat, carry your bird on the falconer's glove, fly it at your quarry and it brings back the catch. Falconry lure and hood.
- **Contests:** the contest horn starts a five-minute falconry contest or, sneaking, a bird race, with a sidebar scoreboard and a Golden Raptor trophy for the winner.
- **Birdwatching gear:** binoculars (zoom; sightings with distance), Bird Guide, numbered bird rings, bird hide, nest boxes, bird call and owl pellets.
- English and Danish.
